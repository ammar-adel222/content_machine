Privacy Policy

Application: The Byte Canvas (Content Machine) · Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Overview

The Byte Canvas (Content Machine) is a private creator publishing automation tool operated exclusively by the Facebook Page and Instagram account owner (ammar2006326@gmail.com). It is used solely to publish original short-form educational videos and Reels and to retrieve aggregate content performance metrics for channels owned and administered by the operator.

2. Information Accessed and Used

When authorized by the account owner, the application uses Meta Graph API permissions strictly to:

  • Publish video files, captions, and Reels metadata to the owner's connected Facebook Page and Instagram Professional account.
  • Read aggregate post and Reel metrics (such as views, plays, likes, and comment counts) for media published to the owner's channels.

The application does not provide public user registration, third-party Facebook Login for external users, or consumer tracking. It does not collect, harvest, or store personal data from public viewers, page visitors, or third-party users.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Owner-authorized API access tokens and channel identifiers are stored solely in the owner's private local environment.
  • The application maintains no remote user database, no analytics cookies, and no advertising or tracking pixels.
  • No data accessed through the Meta Graph API is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or data brokers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Access tokens and local channel catalog logs remain strictly under the owner's control and may be revoked or deleted at any time. Full removal instructions are published on our User Data Deletion Instructions page.

5. Contact Information

For questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling practices, contact the operator at ammar2006326@gmail.com or open an issue at https://github.com/ammar-adel222/content_machine/issues.
